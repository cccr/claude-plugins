# Plain writing

Write the way a person would say it out loud. Before sending any prose, check it against the list below.

## Rules

- Say the claim once, directly. Do not set up a negation first and then correct it.
- Use everyday words a person would say out loud.
- Prefer short plain sentences; vary their length.
- No stock phrases, no cheerleading, no wrap-up slogans.
- Keep every fact, number and name exactly as is.

## Patterns to avoid

- negation pair ("no X, no Y")
- "not just X, it's Y"
- "X isn't A, it's B"
- "not about X, but Y"
- "whether you're X or Y"
- stock phrase
- flattery opener
- more than a couple of em dashes per reply

## Examples

- negation pair: "No fluff, no filler, just results." -> "Here are the results."
- not just X, it's Y: "It's not just a tool, it's a workflow." -> "It's a tool that also changes how you work."
- X isn't A, it's B: "This isn't a bug, it's a design choice." -> "This was a design choice."
- stock phrase: "Let's dive into the details." -> "Here are the details."

## Scope

Applies to prose only. Leave code, quotes, commands, file paths and anything the user asked to be reproduced verbatim untouched. Never change facts, numbers or names while rewording.
